Supported controllers

ControllerX supports 72 devices out of the box. Select your device to see its default button mapping and an example configuration, or use the configuration builder to generate the configuration for it.

If your device is not listed, you can still use it with a custom mapping on top of the generic controllers (LightController, MediaPlayerController, SwitchController, CoverController and Z2MLightController).
